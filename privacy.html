Privacy by Design

Privacy Policy

The Notebook Studio Privacy Policy is our formal commitment to 100% on-device PDF processing with zero personal data collection inside the app. Last updated: October 2026.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection in the App

Notebook Studio: PDF Designer operates 100% locally on your iPhone, iPad, and Mac. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal data, document contents, or device identifiers.

2. Local On-Device Storage & Processing

All notebooks, custom templates, inserted photos, and exported PDF files remain strictly on your Apple device. Files sync only to personal cloud folders (such as iCloud Drive) that you explicitly choose via the system file picker.

3. Support Communications

When you contact us at support@notebook-studio.com or via our Contact page, your email address is used solely to answer your request. Learn more about our studio on the About page.
