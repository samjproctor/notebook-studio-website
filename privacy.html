Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

Notebook Studio: PDF Designer is engineered to operate 100% locally on your iPhone and iPad. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data, usage analytics, telemetry, or device identifiers.

2. Local On-Device Storage & Processing

All notebooks, templates, photos you choose to insert, and exported PDF files remain strictly on your device or in the personal cloud storage folders (such as iCloud Drive) that you explicitly select via the standard iOS/iPadOS system file picker.

3. Support Communications

If you contact us directly via email at support@notebook-studio.com, your email address and message contents are used solely to reply to your inquiry and provide technical assistance.
