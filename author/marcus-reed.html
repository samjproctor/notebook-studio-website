Marcus Reed

Marcus Reed is the Lead e-Ink Hardware Reviewer at Notebook Studio, responsible for physical PPI calibration and tablet toolbar safe-zone verification. He benchmarks exported vector PDFs across reMarkable Paper Pro (229 PPI), Paper Pro Move (264 PPI), reMarkable 2 (226 PPI), and Amazon Kindle Scribe (300 PPI).

Every device guide and habit tracker specification published on notebook-studio.com is verified by Marcus on physical e-Ink panels using native stylus hardware and finger-tap link audits. His testing ensures 0.5pt hairlines render without raster aliasing and side tabs never collide with system toolbars.
