Notebook Studio Editorial Team

Elena Vance

Elena Vance is the Senior Digital Stationery Editor at Notebook Studio, specializing in hyperlinked PDF planner architecture and e-Ink typography. She designs and tests daily, weekly, and monthly vector planner systems for reMarkable Paper Pro, reMarkable 2, Supernote, and iPad.

Before joining Notebook Studio in 2026, Elena spent six years designing grid systems, modular habit trackers, and print-to-digital stationery workflows in London. Her work focuses on reducing page-flipping friction through automated Year-Month-Week-Day PDF link hierarchies and 1:1 millimeter grid calibration.
