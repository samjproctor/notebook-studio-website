Quick Transfer Guides by Device

reMarkable 2 & Paper Pro

Export your PDF in Notebook Studio and select "Share to reMarkable" in the Share Sheet. You can also upload files at my.remarkable.com.

Amazon Kindle Scribe

Share the exported PDF to the Amazon Kindle app or upload via Send to Kindle. All internal finger-tap hyperlinks remain active.

Supernote & Onyx BOOX

Transfer your exported vector PDF using the Supernote Partner App, BOOXDrop, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, or a USB-C cable.
